# Heavy Wall Pounding

- Original file: `448374__lunafarrowe__heavy-wall-pounding.wav`

The repository assets `assets/audio/wall/wall_pound_light.ogg`,
`assets/audio/wall/wall_pound_medium.ogg`, and
`assets/audio/wall/wall_pound_heavy.ogg` are trimmed three-pound variants
converted to normalized OGG. They include a low-end boost and progressively
stronger level gain for the strike warning levels. The clips are time-compressed
so the three impacts land in a quick boom-boom-boom cadence. Each variant is
approximately 0.6 seconds long and has an additional amplitude boost with peak
limiting. A short synthesized low-frequency thump, low-mid EQ boost, and subtle
bit-reduction saturation were added to give the impacts more body and presence.
